Support — Number Match: 10

Number Match: 10 is an offline number puzzle for iPhone by ValleyTale Studios. Tap two numbers that are equal or add up to 10 and are connected by a clear path. Clear rows, use Add when you run out of pairs, and clear the board. Play Classic for a high score, Adventure for levels and stars, or the Daily challenge.

Contact

Email valleytalestudios@gmail.com. We answer in English or Swedish. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the app version (bottom of Settings) and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

How do I play?

Open Settings → How to Play for an interactive tutorial.

Where is my progress saved?

The game saves progress on your device and keeps a second copy in local app storage. The game has no account or in-app sync.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Delete game data and tap twice to confirm. This clears saved games, progress, best score, settings, and their local backup.

Does the game need the internet?

No. Every mode works fully offline. Ads and rewarded videos need a connection; without one, the game simply shows none.

Privacy

This page describes the ad-supported iOS release. An ad-free build without the advertising SDK shows no ads and collects no data. The ad-supported game is free and supported by non-personalised Google AdMob ads (npa=1). It has no account and no in-app purchases. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you can manage your consent in Settings → Privacy choices. The app never shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt in those regions, or anywhere a consent or Privacy choices answer has already been recorded on the device, and it never accesses the advertising identifier (IDFA) there. Outside those regions, and only if no such choice has been recorded, the app may show that prompt once. You can change or revoke tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads may still appear as non-personalised ads. Read the Number Match: 10 Privacy Policy.
