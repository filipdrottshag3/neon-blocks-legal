Privacy Policy — Number Match: 10

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Who we are

Filip Drottshage, operating as ValleyTale Studios in Sweden, is the data controller for Number Match: 10 on iOS. For privacy questions, email valleytalestudios@gmail.com.

2. Summary

Number Match: 10 is an offline puzzle game. It has no account, no in-app purchases, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for permission to track you, and it does not collect personal data. ValleyTale Studios receives nothing from the app.

3. Game data on your device

The game stores the following on your device only, so you can continue where you left off:

DataWhy
Current Classic, Adventure and Daily gameResume your game
Best score, level progress, stars, daily streakShow your progress and unlock backgrounds
Settings (sound, haptics, game speed, background)Remember your choices
A short local diagnostic log (app start, going to and from the background, errors)Help you describe a problem if you contact support; it never leaves the device unless you send it to us

To protect your progress, the game keeps a second copy of these saves and settings in its own app storage on the same device. This data is not sent to us or anyone else. iOS may include app data in device backups according to your settings. To reset data in the installed app, open Settings → Delete game data and tap twice. The reset clears game data and its on-device backup. It does not modify existing iOS device backups.

4. If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and what you send only to answer your request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing support (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Our support mailbox is hosted by Google. Contact us if you have questions about a support message or want to request its deletion.

5. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data we hold about you, and object to processing. Since the app sends us nothing, this normally only concerns emails you have sent us. Contact valleytalestudios@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority or the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

7. Changes

If a future version adds anything that processes personal data (for example optional rewarded videos), we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date before that version is released, and ask for your consent in the app where the law requires it.
