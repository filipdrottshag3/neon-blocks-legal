1. Who we are

Filip Drottshage, operating as ValleyTale Studios in Sweden, is the data controller for Number Match: 10 on iOS. For privacy questions, email valleytalestudios@gmail.com.

2. Summary

This privacy policy applies to the ad-supported iOS release of Number Match: 10. An ad-free build of the game does not include the advertising SDK: that build shows no ads and collects no data. On-device game saves, described in section 3, exist in both builds. Advertising in sections 4 and 6 applies only to the ad-supported release.

The ad-supported release is a free puzzle game funded by advertising. It has no account, no in-app purchases and no analytics of its own, and the game itself sends nothing to ValleyTale Studios. Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (13.11.0) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK (3.1.0), and they are requested as non-personalised. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your choice in Google's consent form before any ad is requested. The app never shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt in those regions, and never accesses the advertising identifier (IDFA) there, whether you consent, decline or customise your choices. Ads may still appear as non-personalised ads (npa=1). On iPhone, Apple's tracking prompt is shown only where Google's consent form is not required and no consent or Privacy choices answer has ever been recorded on the device.

3. Game data on your device

The game works fully offline. It stores the following on your device only, so you can continue where you left off. These saves do not need a connection, and the game itself does not send them to ValleyTale Studios or anyone else:

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Advertising in the ad-supported release uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (13.11.0) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK (3.1.0).

Where ads appear

The app shows full-screen interstitial ads at natural breaks only: Game Over (after a lost board) and Next Stage (after you clear a board). Cooldown and pacing decide whether an interstitial is shown at that break, so one does not appear every time. Add, including the third Add on a board, leaving a game, and restarting a game are not ad breaks. The app does not show banner ads or App Open ads.

You can choose to watch an optional rewarded video for +3 Hints, +3 Undo, or Continue after Game Over. The game grants that single reward only after Google confirms the video was completed. Closing the video early does not grant it.

Ads and rewarded videos need a connection. Offline, every mode still works and the game shows no ads.

How ad requests are made

Interstitial and rewarded ads are requested as non-personalised (npa=1). Before the Google Mobile Ads SDK is initialised, the app turns Publisher First-Party ID off. Turning that identifier off does not stop the processing described below. Non-personalised ads are not based on your past behaviour. They still process device identifiers for frequency capping, aggregated ad reporting, measurement and fraud prevention.

Permission to track on iPhone

Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt is shown only where Google's consent form is not required and no consent or Privacy choices answer has ever been recorded on the device. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, or anywhere you have already answered Google's consent form or Privacy choices, the app never shows the ATT prompt and never accesses the advertising identifier (IDFA). Where the prompt is shown, it appears so Google can measure ads and limit repeats. If you allow tracking, Google's SDK may access the IDFA for ad measurement, frequency capping and fraud prevention. Number Match: 10 itself never reads or stores the IDFA. If you decline or restrict tracking, or if the prompt is not shown, the game remains fully playable. Ads may still appear as non-personalised ads (npa=1). You can change or revoke tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Data the advertising SDKs process

The types below match the privacy manifests shipped with Google Mobile Ads SDK 13.11.0 and Google User Messaging Platform SDK 3.1.0, which we inspected in the official 13.11.0 and 3.1.0 SDK packages. ValleyTale Studios does not receive or store this SDK data. Google and its advertising partners, listed in the consent form, process it under their own policies. The list of partners is set by that form and can change; this policy does not state a fixed number of partners. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google's Privacy Policy, and Google's App Store data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK 13.11.0 privacy manifest already declares coarse location as collected and linked to you, and not used to track you. Google's disclosure also includes coarse location derived from the IP address, which does not require a location permission. Number Match: 10 does not ask for location permission, does not use the device's GPS or Approximate Location Services, and does not collect precise location from the device. Google's consent form includes a standard sentence that we and our partners may use precise geolocation data; that wording is part of Google's form and is not a setting this app can turn off. This app still does not request or collect device precise location. Any partner processing is described in the consent form and in those partners' own policies. From version 13.12.0, Google's SDK can also collect approximate location from the device if an app already has location permission; this app stays on 13.11.0 and would review its privacy labels and this policy before any SDK upgrade.

Legal basis and your choices

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform consent form and waits for your choice before any ad request. The app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt in those regions, whether you consent, decline or customise your choices, and it never accesses the IDFA there. The legal basis is your consent under GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and the ePrivacy rules on storing or reading information on your device. If you decline, the game remains fully playable. The app requests an ad only when Google's consent result allows it; depending on that result, you may see no ads, or only limited non-personalised ads (npa=1). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices. After any choice in the consent form or Privacy choices, the tracking prompt is never shown, including on later launches or if you later travel to a region where Google's consent form is not required. Withdrawing consent applies to later ad requests.

Transfers and retention

Google may process advertising data outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, including in the United States, under its own safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses. Google decides how long it keeps that data, as described in its policies. ValleyTale Studios does not receive or store it and does not set a retention period for it. Deleting game data in the app, or deleting the app, removes the on-device saves described in section 3. It does not delete data Google already holds; for that data, use Google's own tools or contact Google.

5. If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and what you send only to answer your request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing support (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep a support message only as long as needed to handle your request. Our support mailbox is hosted by Google, which may process it outside the EEA under its own safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses. Contact valleytalestudios@gmail.com if you have questions about a support message or want to request its deletion.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children and is not a children's service. Ads are requested as non-personalised and are not tagged as child-directed.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and the right to object to processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it. You can withdraw advertising consent in the app under Settings → Privacy choices, and you can change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The app sends ValleyTale Studios nothing from gameplay, so a request to us normally concerns emails you have sent us. For data Google processes for advertising, you can also use Google's own tools and contact Google. To use these rights with us, email valleytalestudios@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a local supervisory authority or to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

8. Changes

If we change how the app processes personal data, we will update this policy and the date above before that version is released, and ask for your consent in the app where the law requires it.
