Privacy Policy — Number Match: 10

Last updated: [[RELEASE DATE of the ad-supported version]]

1. Who we are

Filip Drottshage, operating as ValleyTale Studios in Sweden, is the data controller for Number Match: 10 on iOS. For privacy questions, email valleytalestudios@gmail.com.

2. Summary

Number Match: 10 is a free puzzle game funded by advertising. It has no account, no in-app purchases and no analytics of its own, and the game itself sends nothing to ValleyTale Studios. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and are not personalised. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your choice in Google's consent form before any ad is requested. The app does not ask for permission to track you.

3. Game data on your device

The game stores the following on your device only, so you can continue where you left off:

DataWhy
Current Classic, Adventure and Daily gameResume your game
Best score, level progress, stars, daily streakShow your progress and unlock backgrounds
Settings (sound, haptics, game speed, background)Remember your choices
A short local diagnostic log (app start, going to and from the background, errors)Help you describe a problem if you contact support; it never leaves the device unless you send it to us

To protect your progress, the game keeps a second copy of these saves and settings in its own app storage on the same device. This data is not sent to us or anyone else. iOS may include app data in device backups according to your settings. To reset data in the installed app, open Settings → Delete game data and tap twice. The reset clears game data and its on-device backup. It does not modify existing iOS device backups.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows full-screen ads at natural breaks — after a lost or completed board, when you leave or restart a game, and at the third Add of a board — and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for three more hints, three more undos or one extra Add. A reward is only given when Google reports that the video was watched.

To deliver, limit how often you see the same ad, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK processes, as Google describes it: an app- or developer-scoped device identifier (not the advertising identifier, which the app never asks for), advertising data such as ads you have seen, product interaction such as app launches and video views, performance and crash diagnostics, and coarse location derived from your IP address. Google processes this data under its own responsibility; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

We request non-personalised ads. Non-personalised ads are not based on your past behaviour, but still use identifiers for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.

Legal basis and your choices

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, ads are requested only after you have made a choice in Google's consent form (consent under GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and the rules on storing or reading information on your device). If you do not consent, the app may show no ads or only limited ads; the game remains fully playable either way. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices.

Transfers and retention

Google may process data outside the EEA under its own safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses. Google decides how long it keeps advertising data, as described in its policies. ValleyTale Studios does not receive or store this data.

5. If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and what you send only to answer your request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing support (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Our support mailbox is hosted by Google. Contact us if you have questions about a support message or want to request its deletion.

6. Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children. Ads are requested as non-personalised.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data we hold about you, and object to processing. The app sends us nothing, so this normally concerns emails you have sent us; for data Google processes for advertising, you can also use Google's own tools and contact Google. Contact valleytalestudios@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority or the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

8. Changes

If we change how the app processes personal data, we will update this policy and the date above before that version is released, and ask for your consent in the app where the law requires it.
