Privacy Policy — Sliding Blocks

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Who we are

Filip Drottshage, operating as ValleyTale Studios in Sweden, is responsible for the processing we describe in this policy for Sliding Blocks on iOS. For privacy questions, email valleytalestudios@gmail.com.

2. Game data on your device

Sliding Blocks is an offline puzzle game. Classic keeps your current game and best score. Adventure keeps your level progress and stars. Achievements, hint balance, game settings and a local diagnostic log are also stored on your device. The game has no account or in-app purchases. ValleyTale Studios does not receive your saved games or local diagnostic log. Local data stays in the app until you delete it and may be included in device backups, depending on your iOS settings.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

Google AdMob supplies optional rewarded ads for hints or Continue, and occasional ads between games or levels. Whatever you choose, the app requests only non-personalised ads. It also turns off Google’s publisher first-party identifier.

On iOS, the app may ask through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt for permission for Google to use your device’s advertising identifier to measure ads and limit how often you see the same ad. Where the privacy form described below applies (for example in the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland), the prompt can appear only after you have made your choices in that form, and only if you allowed storing or accessing information on your device. Where no such form is required, the prompt may appear before the first ad. The prompt is not shown when you refused that consent choice, when a privacy choice is still required or could not be read, when ads cannot be requested, or when you have already answered it. You can decline, and you can change your answer later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not available to the app or to Google. The game remains playable, and ads may still appear. Allowing tracking does not switch the app to personalised ads.

Google Mobile Ads may process your IP address and approximate location, device identifiers, ad views and interactions, other app interactions, and crash and performance information. Google uses these categories for purposes such as delivering and measuring ads, improving service performance, and detecting fraud. Google and eligible ad technology partners may receive information as part of ad delivery and measurement. Where shown, the consent form lists the partners and purposes and identifies any processing based on legitimate interest.

Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) updates your consent status and, where applicable, presents privacy choices. In the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, the consent form lets you accept, decline or manage options when it is presented. In Settings → Privacy choices, when available, you can review or withdraw consent and object to purposes offered under legitimate interest. Where consent is required, it is the basis for those purposes. Security and fraud prevention may rely on legitimate interests where applicable. Your choices govern what ad processing is allowed. Depending on your choices and region, eligible limited ads may still appear. The game remains playable regardless of your choices.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

4. Support and retention

If you contact us, we use your email address and the information you send to answer your request and troubleshoot issues. We rely on our legitimate interest in providing support and retain correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations. Google’s retention periods depend on the type and purpose of the data. See Google’s retention information.

5. International transfers

Google may process information outside your country, including outside the EEA. Google describes the safeguards it uses, including adequacy mechanisms and standard contractual clauses where applicable, in its data transfer frameworks. You can contact us for information about safeguards applicable to our processing.

6. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, or object to certain processing. You can withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact valleytalestudios@gmail.com to exercise your rights with us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority or the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
