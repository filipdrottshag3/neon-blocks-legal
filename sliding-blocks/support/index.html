Support — Sliding Blocks

Sliding Blocks is an offline puzzle game for iPhone by ValleyTale Studios. Slide blocks sideways and clear full rows. In Classic, play for a high score. In Adventure, complete level objectives and earn stars.

Contact

Email valleytalestudios@gmail.com for help. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version from Settings and what you were doing when the issue occurred. If a reward was missing, tell us whether it was for hints or Continue.

Progress and playing offline

Your progress is saved locally. Deleting the app can remove your saved games, scores, stars and achievements. You can play Classic and Adventure offline. Rewarded ads require a connection and an available ad.

Privacy choices

To review or change your ad privacy choices, open Settings → Privacy choices when the option is available. Read the Sliding Blocks Privacy Policy for details.
