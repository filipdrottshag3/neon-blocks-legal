Privacy Policy — Neon Blocks

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the Neon Blocks mobile game for iOS ("Neon Blocks", "the game"), why, on what legal basis, and what choices you have.

The controller responsible for this processing is Filip Drottshage, operating as ValleyTale Studios, Sweden.

Privacy contact: valleytalestudios@gmail.com

2. Information we process

You do not need to give us your name, email address or phone number to play.

Pseudonymous player account

When Neon Blocks first starts with an internet connection, it automatically creates a player account with our backend provider Supabase, without any registration. The account is identified only by a random identifier (your Player ID). It does not contain your name or contact details, but because data is linked to it, it is pseudonymous rather than anonymous, and we treat it as personal data.

You can see and copy your Player ID in the game under Settings → Account & Support → Player ID. If the game has never been online on your device, no account exists and nothing about you is stored on our servers.

Leaderboards and events

If you use the leaderboards or events, we store, linked to your Player ID:

  • the leaderboard name you choose (picked from a predefined list of words; free text is not possible)
  • scores you submit, and identifiers and timestamps of the game sessions behind them (used to prevent cheating)
  • event rewards you have claimed, and related timestamps

Technical data

When the game communicates with Supabase, Supabase receives technical data necessary to deliver the service, such as your IP address, the time of the request and request metadata. This data appears in the service provider's operational and security logs. Supabase's sign-in service can also record the IP address and the app's user agent (technical information about the app and device software) of a sign-in together with your Player ID, to keep accounts secure. We do not use this data to determine your location, to identify you, or for advertising.

Information stored only on your device

Your game progress, coins and other in-game currencies, settings, notification preferences and local diagnostic information are stored on your device and are not sent to us. A loading report can be copied from the game only if you choose to copy it and send it to us yourself.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example, for daily rewards and events) locally on your device. Scheduling them does not send any data to us. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.

3. Advertising and tracking

Neon Blocks can show optional rewarded video ads in versions where advertising is enabled. Earlier ad-free versions do not request ads. An ad only plays when you tap a button that says what you get (for example coins or an extra life). There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and Switzerland, otherwise Google LLC).

When an ad is requested or shown, Google's Mobile Ads SDK receives information such as your IP address (used to estimate your approximate location), device and app information, how you interact with the ad, and diagnostic data. Google uses it to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires it, we ask for your choice through Google's consent message before ads are personalised. When Google's consent tool requires it, Settings → Ad Privacy appears in the game and reopens Google's consent message, where you can change or withdraw your choice at any time. Google UMP records your choices, and the app checks whether ads may be requested before starting the advertising SDK. If you decline personalised advertising or other required consent, Google may serve eligible non-personalised or limited ads, or no ad may be available. Non-personalised ads can still require consent for device storage and measurement. You can continue playing when ads are unavailable; an ad reward is given only after a completed rewarded ad is confirmed by the SDK.

We do not ask for permission to track you through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. Without the required platform permission, the advertising SDK cannot access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). Other app or device identifiers, advertising data and interactions may still be processed by Google. Absence of IDFA does not by itself mean that processing is not tracking under Apple's definition. Your consent choices and applicable platform permissions determine what processing is permitted; a GDPR consent choice does not grant App Tracking Transparency permission. How Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The game stores a record of completed purchases on your device so that items can be delivered. Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

7. How long we keep data

  • Player account, leaderboard and event data: for as long as it is needed to provide these game features, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Session identifiers used against cheating: for as long as they are needed to verify the scores and rewards they belong to.
  • Technical log data at Supabase: for the period for which the provider keeps its operational and security logs.
  • Correspondence with us: for as long as needed to handle your request, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Data on your device: until you delete the game.
  • Backups: our backend provider makes regular backups of the database. After we delete your data, copies can remain in those backups for a limited period until they are automatically replaced.

8. Your rights and how to request deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability.

To delete your account and the data linked to it: open Settings → Account & Support → Delete Account in the game and confirm. This immediately deletes your player account and the leaderboard, event and related data linked to it from our servers, and signs this device out. After that, the game does not create a new account unless you submit a score or claim an event reward.

If Delete Account is not available (for example when you are offline), open Settings → Account & Support → Request account deletion. This opens an email to us with your Player ID filled in. Alternatively, copy your Player ID from Settings and email it to valleytalestudios@gmail.com. Because the account has no name or email, the Player ID is how we find it; we may ask for additional information that only the account holder would know, such as the leaderboard name in use.

We will respond within one month, as required by the GDPR.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Sweden this is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), https://www.imy.se.
